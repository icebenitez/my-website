Founders say they want speed. What they actually want is momentum they can trust.

## Two-Speed Product

| Layer | Change frequency | Risk tolerance |
| ----- | ---------------- | -------------- |
| Marketing pages | Daily | High |
| Core flows | Weekly | Medium |
| Billing/auth | Quarterly | Near zero |

## The Trust Budget

Every launch spends from a trust budget. Ship small, ship reversible, and top the budget up with boring reliability.

- Feature flags over rollbacks
- Kill switches on anything with a queue
- Decimal-point changes to pricing are never "small"

## Ergonomics Beat Heroics

A team that can deploy on a Friday afternoon isn't brave. It's disciplined.
