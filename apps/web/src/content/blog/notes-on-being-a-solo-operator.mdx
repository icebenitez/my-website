Working solo is a scheduling problem disguised as an identity problem.

## Protect The Mornings

Deep work before lunch. Calls after. Non-negotiable.

## Scope In Days, Not Sprints

A solo week is smaller than you think: one build, one review, one write-up.

- Monday: plan, nothing else
- Midweek: the hard 20%
- Friday: ship, write, invoice

## Rest Is Capacity

Burnout is a hardware failure, not a schedule slip. Walks without a phone count as strategy sessions.

> The goal is a pace you can hold for a decade, not a quarter.
