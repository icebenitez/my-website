The stack decisions small teams postpone become the taxes they pay at Series A.

## The First Ten Decisions

- One database, not none
- A deploy pipeline, day two
- Logging inbox-zero from the start

A technical co-pilot is not a contractor. It's an engineer who argues with you about scope and then takes the keys.

## What The Role Looks Like

1. Own the architecture end to end
2. Say no to the roadmap's nice pile
3. Keep the prototype as production, not as debt

## A Simple Test

If your founding team can't give a confident answer to "who owns reliability?" — you need a co-pilot.
