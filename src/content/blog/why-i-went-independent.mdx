After years of building products within companies, I decided to go independent. Here's why.

## The Appeal of Product Engineering

I've always enjoyed the full lifecycle of building software - from the fuzzy early stages of an idea, through architecture and implementation, to shipping and iterating in production. In a traditional role, you often get siloed into one part of that process.

As an independent product engineer, I get to own the whole journey.

## What I'm Looking For

I'm not looking to replace a 9-to-5 with freelance hours. I want to:

- Work with startups that need technical co-pilots
- Build products that solve real problems
- Have ownership over the technical decisions
- Work with a variety of tech stacks and challenges

## The Backend Focus

My expertise is in backend systems - APIs, databases, internal tools. These aren't glamorous, but they're the backbone of most successful products. I enjoy building systems that are:

- Reliable and maintainable
- Well-documented and easy to hand off
- Designed to scale with the business

## What's Next

I'm currently building out my portfolio with personal projects that demonstrate my approach to product engineering. Each project becomes a case study showing not just the code, but the thinking behind it.

If you're a startup founder looking for a technical partner, or a company that needs help building your next system, let's talk.
